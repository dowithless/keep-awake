## App operation

Keep Awake is a local Mac menu bar utility that prevents idle sleep without requiring an app account. The app does not transmit personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports or purchase records to the developer. It has no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, cloud sync or developer backend.

The app uses macOS power-management APIs and sleep/wake notifications. It does not read screen contents, keyboard input, browsing history, file contents or location to provide this function.

## Local settings and logs

The display-awake preference is stored locally using UserDefaults. An active keep-awake session is not saved across launches. macOS manages login items; the app registers or unregisters them only in response to user action and reads their status to update its menu.

Some operation failures may be written to local system logs. The app has no log-upload feature. System-log retention and sharing of diagnostics with Apple depend on macOS settings and Apple's policies. Quitting does not erase saved preferences or unregister a login item you enabled. You can disable launch at login in the app or in System Settings.

## Purchases and Apple services

The Mac App Store version is a one-time paid download with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple handles purchases, payments, downloads and updates. The developer does not receive payment-card details. The app does not verify purchase receipts or access transaction records.

Apple services and store reports made available to developers are separate from the app's own data processing and are covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and applicable service terms.

## Retention, control and deletion

The app has no developer-operated user accounts or user database. Local preferences can be reset by deleting the app's local settings. Uninstalling does not guarantee that macOS removes all preferences or system logs.

The developer retains voluntarily received information only as needed to handle support or privacy requests, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations, then deletes or anonymizes it. You may email requests for access, correction, cessation of optional processing or deletion. We handle requests under applicable law and verify the requester's identity only when necessary.

Public GitHub content can be edited or deleted using platform features, and you can contact the developer for assistance. The developer cannot guarantee removal of public material already copied by others. GitHub, Google and Apple manage their platform logs, backups and account data under their own policies. If information must legally be retained, we will explain why it cannot be deleted immediately.
